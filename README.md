# Price From Costing

Bakery costing calculators. Enter the costing and get the selling price per piece.

- **Price From Costing** (this folder): product tabs, pack-price ingredients, markup or margin, tax and rounding.
- **Simple Bake Costing** (`simple/`): step by step, covering ingredients, electricity, water and microwave, delivery, and optional pay for your time.

Open either one on an iPhone in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. After it has been opened once with internet, it works offline.

Everything you type is saved only on your own device.
