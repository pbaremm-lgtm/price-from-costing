Markup or margin: which one?

Markup is your profit compared with your cost. Margin is your profit compared with the selling price. The same cupcake can have a 50% markup and a 33.3% margin, so check which one you mean.

Markup: price = cost × (1 + markup ÷ 100)

Margin: price = cost ÷ (1 − margin ÷ 100)

Tax goes on after that: customer pays = price × (1 + tax ÷ 100)
